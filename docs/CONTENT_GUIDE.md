# Content Guide

## Canonical identity

Use:

> CoreG is an authorized intermediary serving private capital markets.

Do not describe CoreG itself as the governance platform, governance infrastructure, Trust Infrastructure owner, or technology provider.

## Canonical purpose

> CoreG exists because private capital transactions require an intermediary whose responsibility extends beyond transaction execution to ensuring every transaction is conducted with confidence, integrity, and accountability.

## Operating standard

> Truth in Transactions is our operating standard.

The supporting sequence is:

> Every identity. Every credential. Every authority. Every document. Every material claim.

> Independently verified before a transaction can advance.

> If a claim cannot be independently verified, it does not become part of the transaction.

## Who We Serve taxonomy

1. Capital Providers
2. Transaction Principals
3. Transaction Participants
4. Digital Asset Participants

Entity types may appear under more than one role depending on the transaction. The taxonomy is based on role, not legal form.

## Governance ownership

Use language that makes the distinction explicit:

- CoreG conducts intermediary activity under a governed operating standard.
- CoreIdentity Technologies owns and operates Trust Infrastructure and Autonomous Execution Governance.
- Governance supports CoreG's transaction activity; it is not CoreG's separate product offering.

## Regulatory caution

Avoid statements that imply:

- CoreG participation provides or substitutes for a license.
- A transaction has regulatory approval merely because it enters the process.
- Verification guarantees close, settlement, valuation, legitimacy, or commercial performance.
- Non-licensed participants may perform regulated activities.

## Digital assets

Digital asset content may reference supported capabilities such as wallet validation, provenance analysis, custody validation, risk indicators, forensic analysis, and API connectivity with supported institutions. Avoid presenting all capabilities as universally available for every asset, custodian, jurisdiction, or transaction.
