# CoreG Engineering Journal

## 2026-07-30 — v3.0.0 institutional baseline

CoreG's website moved from iterative page editing to a governed release model. Version 3.0.0 establishes the public site as a versioned institutional software asset.

### Decisions

- CoreG is positioned first as an authorized intermediary serving private capital markets.
- CoreIdentity Technologies owns and operates the underlying Trust Infrastructure and Autonomous Execution Governance capabilities.
- CoreG operates under that governance; it is not positioned as the owner of the governance discipline.
- Truth in Transactions is the operating standard.
- Participant categories are role-based rather than entity-type-based.
- The homepage functions as an institutional entry point rather than a duplicate of detailed interior pages.
- Navigation must reflow at constrained widths rather than shrink the entire desktop header.

### Canonical hero statement

CoreG facilitates qualifying private capital transactions through a disciplined operating standard built to ensure confidence, integrity, and accountability from first engagement through close.
