# CoreG Website Canon

## Purpose
The CoreG public website presents the Firm as an authorized intermediary serving private capital markets. It is an institutional interface, not a product-marketing site.

## Governing positioning
- CoreG is the operating intermediary.
- CoreIdentity Technologies owns and operates the Trust Infrastructure beneath CoreG.
- Governance, verification, and platform capabilities support the institution; they do not replace the institution as the primary story.

## Canonical navigation
1. Home
2. Who We Are
3. How We Work
4. Platform
5. Leadership
6. About
7. Insights
8. Contact

## Page responsibilities
- **Home:** Introduces CoreG and routes visitors to the appropriate destination.
- **Who We Are:** Defines the Firm, its institutional role, and the participants it serves.
- **How We Work:** Explains the canonical eight-stage transaction process.
- **Platform:** Explains the operating environment and current platform status.
- **Leadership:** Identifies the people accountable for the standard.
- **About:** States mission, vision, purpose, and the CoreIdentity relationship.
- **Insights:** Houses announcements, thought leadership, platform updates, and FAQ.
- **Contact:** Provides the institutional contact path and request-access status.

## Content rules
- One primary responsibility per page.
- The homepage introduces; destination pages explain.
- Do not simplify or rename canonical transaction stages for marketing convenience.
- Do not place platform availability notices near the homepage hero.
- Avoid duplicated explanatory sections across pages.
- Use institutional language before promotional language.

## Platform notice
The following wording is canonical:

> **Platform Enhancement Notice**
>
> The CoreG platform is currently undergoing planned enhancements as part of our commitment to continuous improvement and institutional excellence. During this period, participant onboarding is temporarily unavailable as we complete these enhancements.

The notice may appear on the Platform page and at the Request Access/Contact destination only.

## Release governance
Every public-site change must be evaluated for:
1. fidelity to the CoreG operating model;
2. contribution to institutional confidence; and
3. alignment with this canon.
